Zelensky-claims-that-Russia-is-attacking-Ukraine-with-North-Korean-missiles.

By the EcstacyGlobal team · 8 min read

The war between Ukraine and Russia showed little appetite for sleep overnight, as both sides continued a relentless exchange of strikes, while Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky accused Moscow of turning to North Korea for additional military support.

In the southern Ukrainian city of Zaporizhzhia, seven people were killed and at least 24 others injured in a Russian attack that Ukrainian authorities say involved ballistic missiles supplied by Pyongyang, one of Moscow's increasingly important wartime allies.

The strikes came as Ukraine continued to demonstrate that the battlefield extends well beyond its own borders.

In western Russia, Ukrainian attacks reportedly struck warehouses belonging to Wildberries, the country's largest online retailer — a company often described as Russia's answer to Amazon. Its facilities have come under repeated attack in recent weeks.

The latest exchanges followed one of the deadliest Ukrainian drone assaults on Russian territory in recent months. At least 13 people, including a child, were killed in strikes on the Russian republic of Tatarstan, more than 1,100 kilometres (680 miles) from Ukraine's border.

And in a video address on Monday evening, Zelensky offered a particularly stark assessment of Moscow's reliance on Pyongyang

"For the first time," he said, Russia "cannot wage war without reinforcements from North Korea."

The Ukrainian president argued that the deployment of North Korean missiles and troops was not merely a matter for the battlefield in Ukraine, but a development with consequences far beyond it.
